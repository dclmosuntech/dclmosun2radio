Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

We only collect minimal data necessary for community registration, including first name, last name, phone number, fellowship region, and group/district details.

2. Image Handling

Any profile picture selected from your device is dynamically compressed to under 200KB before being saved into Firestore, keeping app performance fast and completely avoiding external storage tracking.

3. Data Security

Your personal details are stored inside Firebase's secure Firestore servers. Access is strictly controlled through custom Firebase Security Rules tied to your active authenticated session UID.

4. Respect for Privacy

DCLM Osun State II respects digital boundaries. We will never sell, lease, or distribute your registration data to any third-party organizations.

Terms & Conditions

1. Community Platform

DCLM Osun State II mobile app is provided exclusively to support fellowship, church broadcasts, and spiritual study resources among members.

2. Fellowship Chat Room Code

Keep all active chat room conversations edifying, Christ-centered, and respectful. Spamming, advertisement, and unedifying remarks are strictly banned and will result in administrative message purges.

3. Media Content Usage

All audio streams, video broadcasts, and study guides are standard intellectual properties of Deeper Christian Life Ministry. Media files must be used purely for personal study and spiritual growth.
